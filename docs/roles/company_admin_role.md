# StriveWheels: Company Admin Role

## Overview
The `COMPANY_ADMIN` role is a restricted, B2B-focused persona. It is designed for corporate partners who use StriveWheels for their corporate travel needs. Company Admins can only see data and configurations that belong specifically to their organization.

## Key Responsibilities & Access

### 1. Corporate Oversight
- **Dashboard (`/company`)**: A specialized, isolated view showing travel metrics, expenditure, and usage statistics strictly for the company's fleet/employees.

### 2. Account Management
- **Companies (`/company/companies`)**: Manage the top-level corporate account details, billing information, and company-wide travel policies.

### 3. Personnel Management
- **Employees (`/company/employees`)**: Onboard, offboard, and manage corporate employees. Assign travel budgets, limits, or specific ride permissions to individuals.
- **Riders (`/company/riders`)**: Track and monitor the riders associated with the company's account, viewing trip histories to audit corporate travel expenses.
